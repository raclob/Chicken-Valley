CHICKEN VALLEY v0.2 - WINDOWS PC PROTOTYPE

Extract the entire ZIP before starting.
Open ChickenValley.exe and click Start match vs AI.
No Godot installation is required.

WASD / arrow keys - move your farmer
Hold right mouse button - rotate and tilt the camera
Release right mouse - use the menus
Center view - place the camera behind your farmer
Farm & raids - show purchases, upgrades and raid actions
Esc / Pause - pause or resume

Chickens earn coins over time. Buy chickens and coops to expand.
Build fences to stop enemy raids. Boots improve your raiders.
Launch Raid sends a raider to steal enemy chickens and return them.
Most chickens after four minutes wins. Play again restarts the match.

This version is offline against AI. The player farmer explores the farms;
chicken theft is performed by raiders launched from the actions panel.
